Key Insights & Findings
Strategic observations and actionable takeaways from the Amazon India product dataset.
🎯Discount Strategy

57% of products are discounted between 40–80%, indicating a widespread high-discount strategy. Health & Home Improvement categories lead with 53–57% avg discounts.

⭐Quality Signals

63.4% of products maintain ≥4★ ratings despite aggressive discounting. Electronics dominates with 15.8M reviews — 59% of total review volume.

💡Market Concentration

Top 3 categories (Electronics, Computers, Home & Kitchen) account for 97.1% of all products. Amazon Basics products dominate review charts.

🔗Discount vs Rating Correlation
Scatter plot
💡 Key finding: Higher discounts do not strongly correlate with lower ratings. The majority of deeply discounted products maintain 3.5–4.5★ ratings, suggesting discount-driven purchasing is well-supported by product quality.
